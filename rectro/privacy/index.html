Rectro: privacy policy

A puzzle game for Android

Rectro is a puzzle game for Android by Jithin Puthanveetil ("we"). This page says what the game does with your data. In short: the game runs on your phone, we have no servers, and we receive nothing about you.

Last updated: 17 November 2026

What stays on your phone

The game saves these things on your phone only:

  • your progress: finished courses, best cards, stars, coins, your album and Pip's wardrobe;
  • your settings, including the daily reminder if you turned it on;
  • your streak and this week's challenges.

None of this is sent to us or to anyone else. The only data that leaves your phone is what Google collects for a purchase or for an ad you choose to watch, as described below. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its data in Android's settings, deletes it. Backups of the game's data are switched off, so it is not copied to your Google account.

Photos

If you make a course from a photo, you pick the photo with Android's photo picker and the game reduces it to a small pixel picture on your phone. The game keeps up to twelve of these pixel pictures on your phone, until you remove them. The photo itself is not stored by the game and never leaves your phone. The game does not ask for access to your photo library.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the game shows one notification a day when Today's Course is not finished. It is created on your phone; no message is sent over the internet. You can turn it off in the game's Settings or in Android's settings at any time.

Purchases

The full game is a one-time purchase handled by Google Play. Google processes the payment and tells the game only whether the full game is owned. We never see your payment details. Google's privacy policy applies to the purchase: https://policies.google.com/privacy

Ads

Free players can choose to watch an advertisement to get a reward. Ads only play when you tap to watch one; there are no banners and no ads that start by themselves. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. To show and measure ads, Google's ad library collects data from your phone, such as an advertising identifier, your approximate location derived from your IP address, and how you interact with the ad. Where the law requires it, you are asked for your consent first, and you can change your choice under Settings › Ad choices. Owners of the full game see no ads, and the ad library collects nothing from them through ad requests. How Google uses this data: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

What we do not do

  • No account, no sign-in, no email address.
  • No analytics or tracking of how you play.
  • No selling of personal data. We receive none ourselves; the only data that leaves your phone is what Google's ad library collects when you choose to watch an ad (see Ads).

Children

The game is meant for players aged 13 and over and is not directed at children. It has no chat and no user accounts. Ads are limited to content suitable for a general audience.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version is published on this page with a new date.
